Eggs and Bacon Recipe

Home

Description

This recipe shows how to make a eggs and bacon for a great American breakfast

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 cups water
  • bacon

Steps

  1. Crack eggs into bowl. Add a cup of water, and put plenty of salt and pepper in.
  2. Beat eggs with fork until everything is mixed well
  3. Put pan on stove for eggs, and grease with butter. Heat on stove at 150 °
  4. Pour egg mixture into pan, let cook. Flip with spatula to make sure everything is cooked well.
  5. Grease skillet with butter, heat to 350 °
  6. Put on 5 strips of bacon, and cook until well done on each side, flip with spatula
  7. Enjoy your wonderful eggs and bacon. Add more salt and pepper to top it.
