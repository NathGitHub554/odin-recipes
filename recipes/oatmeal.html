Oatmeal Recipe

Home

Description

This recipe shows how to make a warm, lovely oatmeal

Ingredients

  • Oats
  • Milk

    • Steps

    1. Take glass casserole dish, and put 8 half cups of oats.
    2. Pour in 4 cups of milk, and mix around a little
    3. Put the dish in the toaster oven, on bake, 350 °, for 30 minutes.
    4. Take it out and enjoy your warm, delicious oatmeal! Top with honey, maple syrup, cinnamon, peanut butter, or anything else you can think of . . . even cayanne pepper!
