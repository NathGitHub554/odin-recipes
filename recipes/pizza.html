Description

This recipe shows how to make a beutiful, greasy, warm, American-style pizza. This pizza has lots of sauce, a thick blanket of melty cheese, and is topped by pepperoni, chopped bell peppers, and a sprinkling of Italian seasoning. Serve while hot

Ingredients

  • Pita flatbread
  • Shredded mozerella cheese
  • Tomato sauce
  • Bell pepper
  • Pepperoni
  • Italian Seasoning

Steps

  1. Preaheat oven to 350°
  2. Spray pan with non-stick spray, and line with baking sheets
  3. Put your pita on the pan, and use a spoon to spread tomato sauce on thickly
  4. Take the mozerella cheese and sprinkle on till the sauce is completely covered, and can't be seen underneath the cheese
  5. Put pepperoni on top, and sprinkle with Italian seasoning
  6. Dice bell pepper into square pieces, and sprinkle on top of pizza
  7. Put in oven and bake for 15-20 minutes, until cheese melted. You can keep it in longer to brown more if you desire.
  8. Take it out and enjoy your warm, homemade pizza deliciousness!
